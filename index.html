FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during my first consultation?

Your consultation includes a personalized assessment with our doctor. We'll discuss your concerns, goals, medical history, and recommend the most suitable treatment plan for you.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Every patient is unique. During your consultation, our doctor will evaluate your concerns and recommend treatments based on your needs, lifestyle, and desired results.

Are your treatments safe?

Yes. All procedures are performed by trained medical professionals using clinically recognized techniques and high-quality products.

Is there any downtime after treatment?

Downtime varies depending on the procedure. Many treatments have little to no downtime, while some advanced procedures may require a few days of recovery.

When will I see results?

Some treatments provide noticeable improvements within days, while others gradually enhance your appearance over several weeks or months.

Ultherapy Prime

What is Ultherapy Prime?
Non-surgical ultrasound treatment to lift and tighten skin by stimulating natural collagen production.

Which areas can be treated?
Face, neck, and under the chin.

How soon will I see results?
Initial improvement is noticeable, with full results developing over the following months.

Sculptra

What is Sculptra?
Injectable treatment that restores facial volume by stimulating natural collagen production.

How long do results last?
Results develop over months and may last up to two years or longer.

Is Sculptra painful?
Most patients experience only mild discomfort with numbing options available.

Profhilo Structura

What is Profhilo Structura?
Injectable treatment that improves facial structure, skin quality, and firmness.

Who is a good candidate?
Adults with facial volume changes, skin laxity, or early signs of aging.

How many sessions?
Varies per patient. Your doctor will create a personalized plan.

BioRePeel

What is BioRePeel?
Chemical peel that exfoliates skin while improving texture, acne, and fine lines.

Will my skin peel?
Some patients experience minimal peeling, others may have none at all.

Can I return to work?
Yes. Most patients resume normal activities shortly after treatment.

Restylane

What is Restylane?
Dermal filler that restores facial volume and softens wrinkles and folds.

How long do fillers last?
Results typically last several months, depending on the treatment area.

Will I look natural?
Yes. Our goal is balanced, natural-looking enhancements.

Tirzepatide

What is Tirzepatide?
Prescription medication for medically supervised weight management programs.

Is it suitable for everyone?
No. Eligibility depends on medical history and doctor's assessment.

Do I still need diet and exercise?
Yes. Healthy habits remain important for achieving the best results.

Nourkrin

What is Nourkrin?
Clinically researched supplement that supports hair growth and improves hair quality.

Who is it suitable for?
Individuals experiencing hair thinning or looking to support healthier hair growth.

Profhilo

What is Profhilo?
Skin remodelling treatment that improves laxity, hydration, and overall skin quality.

How many sessions?
Typically two sessions, spaced about a month apart.
