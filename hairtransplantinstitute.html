What Causes Hair Loss?

Hair loss can be triggered by a variety of factors including genetics, hormonal changes, medical conditions, and lifestyle. The most common type, androgenetic alopecia (pattern baldness), affects millions of men and women worldwide.

Other causes include chronic stress, nutritional deficiencies (iron, vitamin D, zinc), autoimmune conditions like alopecia areata, and certain medications. In many cases, a combination of factors contributes to hair thinning and shedding.

Understanding the underlying cause is the first step toward effective treatment. At Enhance Hair Transplant Institute, we conduct thorough evaluations—including scalp analysis and medical history—to identify the root of your hair loss and recommend the most appropriate solutions.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FUE hair transplant?

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is a minimally invasive technique where individual hair follicles are extracted and implanted for natural, permanent results.

Is the procedure painful?

Local anesthesia ensures your comfort during the procedure. Most patients experience minimal discomfort.

How long does recovery take?

Most patients return to work within 3-5 days. Full results develop over 6-12 months.

What is PRP therapy?

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy uses your own blood's growth factors to stimulate hair follicles and promote natural regrowth.

Am I a good candidate?

Good candidates include those with pattern baldness, stable hair loss, and sufficient donor hair. A consultation with Dr. Vanguardia will determine your eligibility.

When will I see results?

Transplanted hair typically begins to grow 3-4 months after the procedure, with full results visible at 12-18 months.
